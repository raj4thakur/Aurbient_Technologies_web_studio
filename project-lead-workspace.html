THE PROBLEM

Where the friction starts.

Enquiries arrive through forms, email and spreadsheets. Without a shared process, teams can lose context, duplicate records or miss a follow-up.

A considered approach.

Bring incoming enquiries into one workflow. Validate required details, check for duplicates and route each opportunity using agreed assignment rules. Keep exceptions visible instead of silently dropping them.

What the system could include.

  • Capture enquiries from agreed sources
  • Validate and deduplicate records before CRM entry
  • Assign an owner and create a follow-up task
  • Track processing errors in a review queue

What we would validate first.

Agree on the source tools, CRM access, required fields and assignment rules. Test duplicate handling, failed integrations and manual overrides before introducing the workflow.

The intended outcome.

A connected pipeline designed to give each enquiry a clear owner and make follow-up easier to track.

Specific requirements, feasibility and scope would be agreed before development.
