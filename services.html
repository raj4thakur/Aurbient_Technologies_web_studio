BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

How do we get started?

Share the process you want to improve, the tools you currently use and the people involved. That gives us a starting point for discussing scope and the most useful next step.

Can you work with our existing tools?

We can explore integrations with your existing software. Feasibility depends on its APIs, permissions, data formats and access restrictions; these are checked during scoping.

How much will a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, integrations, complexity and support needs. Once the requirements are clear, we can discuss a defined proposal rather than guessing at a one-size-fits-all price.

How long does a build take?

The schedule depends on the project. We define milestones after reviewing the requirements, technical dependencies and feedback needed from your team.

How do you handle sensitive data and AI accuracy?

Data access, retention and the choice of AI provider should be agreed before development. For consequential workflows, we design review steps and validate results against representative examples.

What happens after launch?

Handover should include documentation, deployment details and an agreed support plan. Ongoing maintenance and improvements can be scoped around the system’s needs.
