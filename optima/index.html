THE PROBLEM IS OFTEN BETWEEN SYSTEMS

Small gaps create
expensive surprises.

Most factories already have information. It is just spread across ERP screens, Excel files, purchase updates, emails and shop-floor conversations. Optima helps connect those signals to the decisions that need to happen next.

01 / MATERIALS↗

Materials are missing when production is ready.

Stock may look fine in isolation, but not against the parts and quantities needed for upcoming work orders.

Optima can helpCompare material availability with planned demand and flag potential shortages early.
02 / PRODUCTION◷

The plan says one thing. The shop floor says another.

Progress updates arrive late or in different formats, so supervisors discover slippage after options have narrowed.

Optima can helpHighlight plan-versus-actual gaps and surface work orders that need a review.
03 / SUPPLIERS⇄

A supplier dependency is easy to overlook.

One unconfirmed delivery or delayed component can quietly affect several orders and promised dates.

Optima can helpTrack supplier-linked dependencies and make follow-ups visible to responsible people.
04 / FOLLOW-UPS✓

Teams spend the day asking for status.

Managers chase updates across calls, messages and spreadsheets instead of working from a shared picture.

Optima can helpCreate a common exception view with clear ownership and next actions.
05 / DELIVERY⌁

Delivery risk becomes clear too late.

Material, production and supplier issues are often assessed separately, even when they affect the same order.

Optima can helpBring related signals together so teams can review orders at risk sooner.
06 / REPORTING▤

Reports explain yesterday, not what needs attention now.

Static reports are useful, but often require someone to notice a problem and manually alert everyone else.

Optima can helpUse configurable rules to highlight exceptions and prompt timely reviews.

Optima supports operational visibility and decision-making. Results depend on source-data quality, agreed rules and how teams use the system; it does not guarantee that every delay will be prevented.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Every deployment is scoped around the use case, available data and the systems involved.

Talk through your setup ↗
Is Optima a ready-made product or custom-built?

It is a configurable manufacturing intelligence product. We tailor workflows, rules, dashboards and integrations to the customer’s needs rather than presenting one fixed setup for every factory.

Do we need to replace our ERP?

No. The goal is to complement existing systems where practical. The integration may use exports, APIs, database access or structured manual inputs depending on what is supported and permitted.

Can we start with Excel files?

Yes, if the files contain consistent, usable data. A spreadsheet-based pilot can help validate the workflow before investing in deeper integrations.

Does Optima automatically control production?

Optima is designed to surface signals and support operational decisions. Any automated updates, notifications or actions are explicitly scoped and depend on system access, rules and customer approval.

How long does implementation take?

Timing depends on the workflow, data quality, number of systems and access requirements. We first scope a focused use case and then agree on milestones; we do not promise a fixed timeline before that review.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the scope of configuration, integrations, user needs and ongoing support. We discuss the workflow first so the proposal reflects the actual implementation.
