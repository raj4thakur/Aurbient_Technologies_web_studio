THE PROBLEM

Where the friction starts.

Teams often retype information from invoices and PDFs into spreadsheets or business systems. Inconsistent layouts make the work slow and difficult to check.

A considered approach.

Extract the fields needed by the business, validate them against agreed rules and present the source document beside the extracted data. Route uncertain or incomplete results to a person before export.

What the system could include.

  • Receive documents through an agreed upload or inbox
  • Extract and validate a defined set of fields
  • Review uncertain results against the source document
  • Export approved records to the destination system

What we would validate first.

Start with representative documents and a clear field schema. Define accuracy criteria, supported formats, data retention and review responsibilities before choosing the extraction approach.

The intended outcome.

A reviewable document workflow designed to reduce retyping while keeping people in control of important records.

Specific requirements, feasibility and scope would be agreed before development.
